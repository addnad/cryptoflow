# Crypto Flow — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-05_

Crypto Flow ("the game", "we") is a mobile game. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written to satisfy the App
Store and Google Play data-disclosure requirements. Publish it at
`https://cryptoflow.game/privacy` before submitting.

## The short version
- We collect only what's needed to run your profile and the leaderboards.
- We do **not** show ads, use third-party trackers, or sell your data.
- There is no real money, cryptocurrency, or trading in the game. The
  "portfolio" is a fictional score.

## What we collect

**Account & identity.** When you sign in we store a Firebase user id (uid).
- *Guest:* an anonymous uid only; data stays tied to that device unless you
  upgrade the account.
- *Google / Apple:* your uid plus the email address associated with that
  sign-in, used to identify your account across devices. Apple's "Hide My
  Email" is fully supported.

**Profile you create.** Username, display name and chosen avatar.

**Gameplay data.** Scores, distance, combos, portfolio values, XP, level,
coins, achievements, daily-reward streak and per-run summaries.

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or
advertising identifiers.

## How we use it
- To create and maintain your profile and progress.
- To power global, weekly and rival leaderboards (your username, display
  name, avatar, level and best scores are visible to other players).
- To sync progress across your devices when you link an account.
- To detect implausible scores and keep leaderboards fair.

We rely on **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions)
as our backend processor. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and
handled under Google's security and privacy terms.

## Data sharing
We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers
or data brokers. The only third party involved is Google Firebase, acting as
our infrastructure provider.

## Your choices and rights
- **Play without an account:** guest mode requires no email.
- **Access / export / delete:** email us to request a copy or deletion of
  your data; deleting your account removes your profile, runs and leaderboard
  entries.
- **Leaderboard visibility:** it reflects the public name and avatar you
  choose — pick a handle you're comfortable showing.

## Children
Crypto Flow is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect
data from children under 13 beyond an anonymous game profile. It contains no
gambling, real-money transactions, or real trading.

## Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last
updated" date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
